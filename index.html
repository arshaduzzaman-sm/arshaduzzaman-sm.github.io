01 — About

Good testing isn't only about finding bugs. It's about giving the whole team the confidence to ship.

I’m an SQA engineer in Tokyo, testing IoT building-automation and energy-management systems end to end — from the devices and firmware on site to the Linux edge gateways, cloud platforms, web apps and REST APIs behind them.

Most of my work is system, regression and load testing. When something fails, I dig into the logs and the network until I can show developers exactly what happened and how to reproduce it. I also write Python tools that make testing faster — my MQTT traffic scripts are now part of our standard load tests.

Before QA, I kept a fully automated sensor production line running as an operating engineer — that is where I learned how much people depend on systems that simply work. Today I work in English and Japanese every day, and I enjoy being the person who connects teams and keeps quality moving.
